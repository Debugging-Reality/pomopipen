YOUR WEEK

Every subject is a tomato.

A calendar of the focus you actually did. Back-to-back rounds of one subject become one block, so a morning of calculus reads as one tall tomato.

  • Real time blocks. Placed by start time, sized by how long you stayed on it; ×3 shows the rounds inside.
  • A variety per subject. Golden, green zebra, plum: told apart at a glance, packed in a basket.
  • Also in Google Calendar. Optional one-way sync into a calendar of its own.
YOUR YEAR

A whole year,
one square a day.

The Charts tab turns every round you log into a heatmap, lifetime totals and trends. Filter it by subject in one click.

  • Year heatmap. Darker squares mean more rounds; hover any day for its count.
  • Totals & streaks. Rounds, focus hours, your best streak and the hours behind each subject.
  • Trends. Daily study time over 30 days, 90 days or a year, with a moving average.
TASKS

A to-do list that knows your subjects.

Tasks live under the subject they belong to. Star one, and every focus round is logged to it.

  • Estimate vs. actual. Say how long you think it takes; the real time fills in as you focus.
  • Check it off. With undo. Finished tasks tuck themselves away.
  • Names on your week. Task titles label the blocks in the calendar.
MAKE IT YOURS

Small when you need it,
yours all the way.

  • Pick a look. Theme, timer style and the print behind your pages.
  • Mini mode. Shrinks to a 240 × 240 timer that keeps phase, time and the main button.
  • Set time your way. Twist the tomato's scale, use the arrow keys, or just type 30.
  • Calm by design. Follows your system's reduced-motion setting; no ticking, no flashing.
