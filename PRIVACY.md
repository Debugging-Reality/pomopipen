# PomoPipen Privacy Policy

PomoPipen is a personal desktop Pomodoro timer. This page describes what happens to data when you use its optional Google Calendar sync.

## What the app accesses

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, PomoPipen asks for these permissions:

- calendar.app.created (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created): lets the app create and manage only the calendar it creates itself ("PomoPipen study log"). It cannot see or change any of your other calendars or events.
- openid and email: used only to show which Google account is connected.

## How data is used

Completed focus sessions (subject, start and end time, duration) are written as events to that calendar. Nothing else is read from your Google account.

## Storage and sharing

- All timer records and the sign-in token are stored only on your own computer, in the app's local data folder.
- The developer runs no server and receives none of your data.
- Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
- Google user data is used only to provide the calendar sync feature described above.

## Revoking access

You can disconnect at any time in the app (Settings, Calendar sync), or remove PomoPipen at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Events already written stay in your calendar until you delete them.
